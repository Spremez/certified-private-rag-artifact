# Data and software credits

* **SciFact.** David Wadden, Shanchuan Lin, Kyle Lo, Lucy Lu Wang, Madeleine van Zuylen, Arman Cohan, and Hannaneh Hajishirzi, *Fact or Fiction: Verifying Scientific Claims*, EMNLP 2020. The authors specify CC BY 4.0 for claims and evidence annotations and ODC-By 1.0 for the S2ORC abstract database. The package contains the dev claims, document identifiers, numerical embeddings, and retrieval measurements. The reproduction script obtains the BEIR corpus from its distributor and reconstructs the reader pages using the published delivery digests. [SciFact data license](https://github.com/allenai/scifact/blob/master/LICENSE.md) · [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) · [ODC-By 1.0](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/by/1-0/)
* **FiQA-2018.** Macedo Maia and colleagues, *WWW'18 Open Challenge: Financial Opinion Mining and Question Answering*, WWW Companion 2018. The BEIR distribution credits the FiQA project and labels its dataset card CC BY-SA 4.0. The release provides numerical embeddings and measurements. The reproduction script obtains the original archive from the BEIR distributor. [FiQA project](https://sites.google.com/view/fiqa/) · [BEIR FiQA data card](https://huggingface.co/datasets/BeIR/fiqa) · [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/)
* **BEIR.** Nandan Thakur, Nils Reimers, Andreas Rücklé, Abhishek Srivastava, and Iryna Gurevych, *BEIR: A Heterogeneous Benchmark for Zero-shot Evaluation of Information Retrieval Models*, NeurIPS 2021. The reproduction uses the byte-exact SciFact and FiQA ZIP distributions recorded in the manifest. [Dataset catalog and upstream downloads](https://github.com/beir-cellar/beir#beir-available-datasets)
* **Microsoft SEAL.** The vendored source retains Microsoft's MIT license and its third-party notices in `sources/SEAL/LICENSE` and `sources/SEAL/NOTICE` in the extracted archive. [Microsoft SEAL](https://github.com/microsoft/SEAL)
* **External inputs.** The artifact guide identifies the sources and expected digests of the MS MARCO/Compass inputs, model weights, RAGtime/Piano code, and their acquisition scripts. Those inputs are acquired from their distributors. The paper and the per-run manifests identify each comparator and model.
